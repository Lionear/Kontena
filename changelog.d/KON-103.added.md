- **"Launch Kontena at login" actually does something now** — on Linux, where Kontena registers itself
  as a startup entry your desktop reads, and removes it again when you switch it off. Until now the
  switch only remembered its own position: nothing was ever written, so nothing ever started. It shows
  the truth rather than our record of it, so disabling the entry in GNOME's or KDE's own startup
  settings is reflected here instead of quietly disagreeing with it, and the switch snaps back if the
  registration did not take. Windows and macOS have their own mechanisms and are not done yet — there
  the row stays hidden, because a switch that cannot work should not be offered. (KON-103)
