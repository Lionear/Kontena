- **A namespace holding only credentials no longer reports itself as empty.** The contents tally on a
  namespace counted workloads, pods, services, ingresses and volume claims, but not config maps or
  secrets — so a namespace whose only contents were credentials answered "Nothing is in this
  namespace", which is the sentence you decide to delete it on. Both kinds are now listed and
  clickable like the rest. What Kubernetes puts in every namespace by itself — `kube-root-ca.crt`
  and service-account tokens — is shown in the counts but left out of the judgement about whether
  the namespace is empty, because counting it would mean no namespace ever is. A namespace whose
  contents Kontena is not allowed to read now says nothing rather than claiming to be empty.
