- **The pod detail now charts CPU and memory over time, not just their current value.** Choose where the charts live under Settings → General → Usage graphs: a sparkline behind the readout in the header, a Metrics tab of its own, or inline above the container table on Overview. Hovering a chart puts a crosshair on the nearest sample and reads out its value. Because `metrics-server` keeps no history of its own, Kontena charts what it sampled while the pod page was open — roughly the last 15 minutes; the longer ranges stay greyed out until a history source such as Prometheus is available.
