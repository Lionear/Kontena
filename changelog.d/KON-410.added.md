- **Put your connections in the order you want.** Each row in the backend switcher has Move up and
  Move down buttons, within its group (engines or clusters). They work from the keyboard too, and
  Kontena remembers the order. A connection added later goes after the ones you arranged.
- **Hide server addresses.** A new switch under Settings › General › Privacy masks where your engines
  and clusters point (sockets, hosts, API servers) in the backend switcher, for screen shares and
  demos. An eye on each row shows that connection's address until the list is redrawn.
